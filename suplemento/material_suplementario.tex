\section{Purpose and Analytical Boundary}

This supplement documents the provenance, treatment, and supporting series used in the manuscript. The aggregate analysis covers the Chilean plastics and plastic-packaging system. Resin-specific eC and IRP calculations are restricted to PET, PP, and PE because these are the categories with consistent coverage across managed material, virgin input, and emission factors. Values for 2024 form the reference assessment. Values for 2025 are a provisional extension and must be interpreted with the treatment information reported below.

\section{Public-Source Audit Protocol}

The audit assessed information publicly accessible up to 15 September 2026. Searches covered governmental and regulatory repositories, the national open-data infrastructure, sectoral associations, and public reports from EPR management systems. Institution names were combined with the Spanish terms \textit{envases y embalajes}, \textit{plásticos}, \textit{resina}, \textit{productos puestos en el mercado}, \textit{recolección}, \textit{valorización}, \textit{reciclaje}, \textit{destino}, \textit{mermas}, \textit{contenido reciclado}, and \textit{cumplimiento REP}. A record was included when it exposed a document, dataset, metadata page, or reporting specification relevant to at least one required variable. Information described as existing inside an authenticated reporting system was classified as non-public unless an open table or downloadable file was also located. \section{Readiness Criteria and Source--Variable--Boundary Inventory}

Readiness is evaluated at the level of a measurement application, not at the level of an institution. \textit{Available} means that all indispensable variables are publicly accessible at compatible boundaries and permit direct calculation without unsupported assumptions. \textit{Partial} means that relevant information exists but requires a disclosed proxy or reconstruction, has incomplete temporal or material coverage, or contains a limited boundary mismatch. \textit{Insufficient} means that at least one indispensable variable cannot be defensibly reconstructed from the public evidence reviewed. The categories are not converted into numbers or averaged.

\endfirsthead
\hline

The sensitivity analysis isolates assumptions introduced by data completion. It does not assign probability distributions to the inputs and therefore does not produce confidence intervals. Each scenario changes one assumption while retaining the remaining reference-case inputs.

For TR$_{packaging}$, estimated recovered packaging is the product of packaging input and the packaging recycling rate. The packaging-share coefficient therefore cancels between numerator and denominator. For IC$_{\mathrm{REP,est}}$, the same cancellation occurs because a time-invariant packaging share is applied to the recovered-volume estimate and to prior-year packaging input. The unchanged ratios are an algebraic property of the implemented model, not evidence that the underlying packaging allocation is known without uncertainty.

\section{Reproducibility Note}

The corrected calculation workbook contains the formulas for TR, $T_{gr}$, IC$_{\mathrm{REP,est}}$, eC, and IRP; a validation sheet compares the manuscript values with the calculated values after rounding. The workbook also records the source and completion method for reconstructed inputs. The manuscript, supplement, workbook, source inventory, verification outputs, and figure-generation script have been prepared for public release at \url{https://github.com/catalinaarriagada9/PAPER_CIMAC}. External source documents are identified by their institutional access points rather than redistributed without an explicit reuse basis. Repository completeness, licensing, and the permanent archived version will be verified before submission.
